# IQLine Website Revamp: From Corporate Template to Product-Led Technical Identity

In late 2024, IQLine underwent a strategic shift. We moved from being a startup establishing a presence to an enterprise-grade healthcare infrastructure company. The existing website, built on a standard WordPress template, had become a liability—it didn't reflect our technical depth, our scale, or our product maturity.

I led the end-to-end redesign and development of the new marketing site, shifting the focus from "generic corporate" to "technical product-led."

## The Challenge: A "Digital Ghost"

The original website at [iqline.co.in](https://iqline.co.in) served as a digital placeholder, but it failed to communicate IQLine's value to sophisticated stakeholders like hospital administrators and government procurement teams.

### Core Drawbacks of the Old Site:
- **Generic Visual Identity:** A light, stock-template design that lacked any distinctive personality. It didn't signal "AI" or "Precision Healthcare."
- **Weak Messaging Hierarchy:** Critical value propositions were buried under generic marketing jargon like *"Elevating Lives Digitally!"*
- **Flat Product Presentation:** Core products like LIMS and MUMS were described in single sentences with no visual differentiation or pathway for deeper exploration.
- **Stat-Heavy but Story-Poor:** Impact numbers (100M+ patients) were presented without context, making them feel like decoration rather than proof of scale.
- **High-Friction Conversion:** CTAs were limited to high-commitment "Book a Demo" actions, with no "middle path" for education-seeking visitors.

---

## The Strategy: Shifting the Narrative

The mission was clear: **Make the website look like the product feels.** 

We chose a technical, dark-themed aesthetic with clinical teal accents. This wasn't just a stylistic choice; it was a positioning move to sit alongside enterprise infrastructure players rather than generic SaaS tools.

### Key Transformation Pillars:

<ProcessPhase title="01. Information Architecture" icon="Layout">
  We restructured the navigation around **User Intent**. Instead of "About Us," we lead with "Products" and "Why Us." We wanted visitors to evaluate our capability before meeting the company.
</ProcessPhase>

<ProcessPhase title="02. Technical Visual Language" icon="Palette">
  Moved from generic system fonts to a technical type scale. Replaced stock photos with data-informed visuals and technical diagrams that reflect the "AI-first" nature of the platform.
</ProcessPhase>

<ProcessPhase title="03. Interactive Credibility" icon="Zap">
  Implemented Framer Motion for scroll-triggered animations and number tickers. This adds a layer of polish and "liveness" that signals a modern, high-functioning engineering team.
</ProcessPhase>

---

## The Result: A Scalable Marketing Engine

The redesigned site at [iqline.netlify.app](https://iqline.netlify.app) (currently in staging/preview) delivers a high-signal experience that earns trust before the first sales call.

### Hero & Credibility
The new hero section immediately names our category and our audience. Below the fold, we turned "Milestone Achievements" into a "Why IQ-Line?" section that pairs massive scale stats with clear, outcome-oriented context.

![New website — Hero Section](/images/projects/iqline-new-hero.png)

### Modern Product Showcase
Each product now has its own identity. We introduced HIMS—our flagship system—to the front-and-center, ensuring our public catalogue matches our actual product roadmap.

### Technical Performance
By moving from WordPress to a React/Vite SPA, we achieved:
- **Instant Transitions:** Near-zero latency between sections.
- **Design System Alignment:** The site uses the same tokens and components as our products, ensuring a seamless transition from "visitor" to "user."
- **Developer Experience:** CI/CD deployment on Netlify allows for rapid content iterations without the bloat of a CMS.

---

## Reflections & Next Steps

This revamp was more than a UI update; it was a declaration of IQLine's maturity. While the current version is a massive leap forward, our roadmap includes:
- **Deep-Dive Product Pages:** Dedicated landing pages for each module with interactive diagrams.
- **Content Strategy:** Transforming the "Blog" into a technical resource hub for healthcare digitisation.
- **SEO & Performance:** Implementing SSR (Server-Side Rendering) for better indexing and further performance gains.
