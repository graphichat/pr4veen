# Sample Transport Tracking Module

Led the design and development of a comprehensive Sample Transport Tracking Module at IQLine that enables healthcare facilities to track medical samples in real-time as they are transferred between locations. The system provides end-to-end visibility, status updates, and ensures sample integrity throughout the transport process. Focused on creating an intuitive interface that reduces errors and improves operational efficiency for healthcare staff managing sample logistics.

## Project Overview

Medical sample transport is a critical but often overlooked aspect of healthcare operations. Samples must be tracked accurately, maintained at proper temperatures, and delivered within specific timeframes to ensure accurate test results. The existing manual tracking systems were error-prone and lacked real-time visibility.

### The Challenge

Healthcare facilities faced significant challenges:
- Manual tracking led to lost or misplaced samples
- No real-time visibility into sample location
- Temperature monitoring was inconsistent
- Chain of custody documentation was incomplete
- Staff spent excessive time on tracking tasks

### The Solution

A comprehensive digital tracking system featuring:
- Real-time GPS tracking of samples
- Automated status updates
- Temperature monitoring integration
- Digital chain of custody
- Mobile app for field staff
- Dashboard for facility managers

## UX Highlights

- **Real-time sample tracking and monitoring** - GPS-enabled tracking with live updates
- **Facility-to-facility transfer workflow optimization** - Streamlined transfer processes
- **Status updates and notifications system** - Automated alerts for all stakeholders
- **Streamlined tracking interface for healthcare staff** - Reduced tracking time by 50%
- **Sample integrity and chain of custody management** - Complete digital documentation

## Design Process

### Discovery & Research

Conducted user research with healthcare facility staff to understand sample transport workflows, pain points, and requirements for tracking systems. We observed operations at 8 different facilities and interviewed 25 staff members involved in sample transport.

**Research Findings:**
- Average of 2-3 samples lost per week per facility
- Manual tracking took 15-20 minutes per sample
- Temperature monitoring was inconsistent
- Staff lacked visibility into sample status
- Multiple systems created data silos

**Deliverables:**
- User interviews (25 interviews)
- Workflow analysis (8 facility workflows)
- Pain point identification (32 issues documented)
- Requirements documentation
- Stakeholder maps

### UX Strategy & Information Architecture

Defined UX strategy for sample tracking workflows, created information architecture, and mapped out user journeys for different user roles. We identified 5 distinct user personas with different needs and workflows.

**User Personas:**
- Lab Technician (sends samples)
- Transport Staff (transports samples)
- Receiving Facility Staff (receives samples)
- Facility Manager (oversees operations)
- Quality Assurance (monitors compliance)

**Information Architecture:**
- Hierarchical organization of information
- Role-based access and views
- Contextual information display
- Progressive disclosure patterns

**Deliverables:**
- UX strategy document
- Information architecture (complete site map)
- User journey maps (5 personas, 12 journeys)
- User personas (5 detailed personas)
- Content strategy

### Wireframing & Prototyping

Created wireframes and interactive prototypes for the tracking module, focusing on real-time updates, status visibility, and ease of use. We prioritized mobile experience since transport staff would primarily use mobile devices.

**Prototyping Approach:**
- Low-fidelity wireframes for all screens
- High-fidelity prototypes for key flows
- Mobile-first design approach
- Offline functionality considerations
- Performance optimization

**Key Flows Prototyped:**
- Sample check-out process
- In-transit tracking
- Sample check-in process
- Status monitoring
- Exception handling

**Deliverables:**
- Low-fidelity wireframes (60+ screens)
- Interactive prototypes (12 key flows)
- User flow diagrams
- Interaction specifications
- Mobile prototypes

### Design & Testing

Developed high-fidelity designs with a comprehensive design system, conducted usability testing, and iterated based on feedback. We tested with actual healthcare staff to ensure the design met real-world needs.

**Testing Results:**
- Task completion rate: 92%
- Average task time reduced by 50%
- Error rate decreased by 70%
- User satisfaction: 8.5/10
- Mobile usability score: 9/10

**Deliverables:**
- Design system (250+ components)
- High-fidelity mockups (80+ screens)
- Usability test reports (3 rounds)
- Design iterations (15 major updates)
- Implementation specifications
